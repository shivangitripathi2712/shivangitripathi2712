# Hi, I am Shivangi Tripathi

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Texas State University. My research focuses on Large Language Model security, hallucination detection and correction, misinformation revision, adversarial robustness, and trustworthy AI.

## Research Interests
- Large Language Models
- Hallucination detection and mitigation
- Paragraph-level misinformation correction
- Adversarial attacks and defenses in LLMs
- Trustworthy and safe AI

## Featured Research Projects

### Long-Output Misinformation Revision
Evidence-grounded sequential revision framework for correcting paragraph-level misinformation in LLM-generated text.

### Hallucination under Adversarial Attack
Analysis of how adversarial prompts can trigger hallucinated behavior in LLMs.
